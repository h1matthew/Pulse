SPEAKER NOTES (Oscar then Felix):

Oscar: Pulse is mobile-first with fluid breakpoints from 375 to 1440 pixels. All elements meet WCAG contrast thresholds. We support dark and light mode. The app is fully keyboard navigable — every action reachable via Tab and Enter. Screen readers receive live announcements through aria-live regions.

Felix: For the intelligent feature, we built a Gemini-powered AI assistant — watch, I type a question and it streams a real-time response using our business data. For input validation, we check on two levels: syntactical — our Zod schemas enforce that ratings are integers 1 through 5, content is 10 to 2000 characters, and IDs are valid UUIDs. Semantic — the server rejects duplicate reviews, verifies CAPTCHA tokens, and auto-computes verified purchase status. Matthew will show both in the demo.

SPEAKER NOTES (Matthew):
Now I will demonstrate all six required features, our customizable report, and our data storage.

SORT BY CATEGORY — On the Discover page, these category pills filter instantly. Food and Drink, Retail, Services — all client-side, no loading delay. We also have a Bookmarks filter here.

LEAVE REVIEWS — On this business page, I write a review, select 4 stars, type my feedback. Notice the Verify CAPTCHA button — I must solve this puzzle before submitting. That is bot verification and input validation working together. The review appears immediately.

SORT BY REVIEWS — The sort dropdown has Highest Rated, Most Reviewed, Nearest, and A to Z. Our default uses a weighted algorithm — rating times the log of review count — so credible businesses rank above single-review outliers.

BOOKMARK FAVORITES — Tapping the heart bookmarks a business. The icon fills instantly with an optimistic update. My Bookmarks page shows all saved businesses.

DEALS AND COUPONS — The Deals page shows active promotions. I claim this deal — it appears in my Claimed tab with a unique redemption code.

BOT VERIFICATION — You already saw the CAPTCHA on the review form. On the login page, users also verify before signing in. The token is checked server-side.

CUSTOMIZABLE REPORT — On the Dashboard, I open the Impact Report. I filter by date range and category. The report has a pie chart, sortable business table, review list, and timeline. I can Download CSV for a spreadsheet or Print Report — notice it switches to a clean white layout with proper formatting.

DATA STORAGE — In DevTools, Application, Local Storage — here is our query cache with a 24-hour TTL. The app loads instantly on return visits. Server-side, Supabase PostgreSQL stores all data in normalized tables with foreign keys.
